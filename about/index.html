About Malek Damiri

Revenue operations and analytics lead in Atlanta, Georgia.

I’m Malek Damiri, Head of Revenue Operations. I live in Atlanta.

My work sits between sales, finance, and data. I own revenue reconciliation, executive dashboards, and the data infrastructure that connects them, and I care most about financial data quality: getting messy, multi-source data to the point where leadership can act on it. Some of that work is here.

Before my current role I spent more than a decade in revenue operations, go-to-market, and supply chain roles at Stenn, Kaseya, Vroom, HelloFresh, and PepsiCo. I started my career in Nashville, spent several years in New York, and now live in Atlanta. I taught myself SQL along the way, and it’s still the first tool I reach for.
